MEDIA ACCESS // MULTIPLE WAYS IN

Accessible Media Matters

Every media resource should clearly identify its access features so students can choose an appropriate way to watch, listen, read, pause, replay, or review the information.

CAPTIONS + TRANSCRIPTS

Videos will be checked for accurate captions or transcripts whenever available.

TEXT ALTERNATIVES

Important information presented only through sound or visuals should have another way to access the meaning.

STUDENT CHOICE

When resources address the same learning need, students can choose among appropriate formats and revisit media as needed.
