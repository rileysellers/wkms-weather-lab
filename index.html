FORECAST PROTOCOL // 01

Follow the Storm Track

Meteorologists build accurate forecasts through structured inquiry. Click each stage for your student checklist!

01

OBSERVE

Examine live weather parameters, cloud formations, and temperature readings.

Checklist:
  • What is the current live air temperature and pressure?
  • Is humidity high enough for cloud/storm formation?
02

QUESTION

Turn your real-time observations into testable scientific research questions.

Checklist:
  • Why is the barometric pressure rising or falling?
  • How does the Atlantic Ocean influence humidity in SC?
03

INVESTIGATE

Gather evidence from databases, books, media, and Doppler radar feeds.

Checklist:
  • Collect data over 24 hours to spot weather fronts.
  • Compare historical severe weather records in Lexington County.
04

VERIFY

Check resource credibility, currency, authority, and cross-reference findings.

Checklist:
  • Is your data feed updating live from NOAA / Weather APIs?
  • Is the author or site an accredited meteorologist?
05

FORECAST

Synthesize evidence to form predictions and create a broadcast presentation.

Checklist:
  • State your claim supported by live weather metrics.
  • Provide safety advice for severe local weather.
