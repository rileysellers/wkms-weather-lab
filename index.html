INQUIRY PROTOCOL // STORM TRACK

Meteorologist Inquiry Flight Plan

Use this forecast protocol as you move through your Meteorologist’s Inquiry Log. Your full research process follows the Stripling Model of Inquiry: Connect, Wonder, Investigate, Construct, Express, and Reflect.

01

OBSERVE

Examine live weather parameters, cloud formations, and temperature readings.

Checklist:
  • What is the current live air temperature and pressure in White Knoll?
  • Is humidity high enough for clouds or rain?
02

QUESTION

Turn your real-time observations into testable scientific research questions.

Checklist:
  • Why is barometric pressure dropping or rising?
  • How does the Atlantic Ocean affect humidity in SC?
03

INVESTIGATE

Gather evidence from databases, ebooks, videos, and Doppler radar maps.

Checklist:
  • Which air mass (cT, cP, mT, mP) is causing the weather event?
  • Find an engineered tech (lightning rod, sea wall, retention basin).
04

VERIFY

Check resource credibility, authority, currency, and properly cite sources.

Checklist:
  • Is your data feed updating live from NOAA / Weather APIs?
  • Did you format references using Station 05 Toolkit?
05

FORECAST

Synthesize evidence into your map, typed script, and live broadcast presentation.

Checklist:
  • State your forecast backed by atmospheric pressure data.
  • Explain safety steps for your target hazard area.
