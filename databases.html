WKMS SOURCE VERIFICATION SYSTEM
Review all five checks before deciding whether evidence belongs in your forecast.
STATUS // UNVERIFIED
A

Authority

Who created it? What makes that person or organization knowledgeable about weather?

C

Currency

When was it published or updated? Does your question require current information?

E

Evidence

What facts, data, observations, or sources support the information?

P

Purpose

Why was it created? Is its goal to inform, persuade, entertain, or sell?

C

Corroboration

Can the important information be confirmed with another reliable source?
