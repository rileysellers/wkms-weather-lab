Use radar evidence when it helps answer your question about precipitation location, intensity, or movement. Explain what the pattern contributes to your forecast.

FIELD QUESTIONWhat does the radar show, and what can you reasonably conclude from that evidence?
FORECAST DESK // BUILD THE VISUAL STORY

Set the Weather Map

Your map is part of the evidence you communicate. Students choose a season and weather situation, then design a map that supports the forecast they will perform.

Broadcast Run of Show

01 // OPENIntroduce the location, season, and current or developing weather situation.
02 // SHOW THE MAPPoint out the atmospheric features your audience needs to understand.
03 // EXPLAIN THE EVIDENCEConnect your researched evidence to the weather pattern and forecast.
04 // FORECASTExplain what weather you expect and why your evidence supports that conclusion.
05 // SAFETY / IMPACTWhen appropriate, explain relevant hazards, impacts, or preparedness information supported by your research.
06 // SOURCESSubmit the typed script with the required references alongside your Inquiry Log and reflection.
