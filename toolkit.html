Keyword Wrench

FIELD INSTRUCTIONS

Turn the question into concepts.

Circle or identify the important nouns and scientific ideas in your inquiry question. Those concepts become your first search terms.

TRY THISInstead of typing your whole question, begin with two concepts such as cold fronts AND thunderstorms.

TOOL 04 // CITATION STAMP

Don't Lose the Source

Students will use the citation style and expectations selected for the final unit. This station focuses first on collecting the information needed to identify a source accurately.

Source Record Card

WHO?Author, organization, or creatorWHAT?Title of the page, article, book, video, or resourceWHERE?Website, database, publisher, or containerWHEN?Publication/update date when availableHOW DID I ACCESS IT?Link, database, call number, or other location information as required

Before You Close the Tab...

1 // RECORD ITCapture the source information while the resource is still open.
2 // CONNECT ITMake it clear which notes or evidence came from that source.
3 // FORMAT ITUse the citation format your teacher and librarian provide for the final script/reference list.
4 // CHECK ITMake sure another person could identify the source from the information you recorded.
